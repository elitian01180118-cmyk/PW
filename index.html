Startup

Progressive Web App · iPhone

Personal Week Planner

A minimal weekly schedule app: upload one screenshot of your timetable, and it shows you what to do right now.

Regular calendar apps never worked for me: they are hard to use and badly laid out. A timetable I design myself in Word works far better. I know exactly what to do, and I actually follow it. So I built an app around that habit. Each week I upload a screenshot of my timetable, an AI model reads it into a table, and the app shows me what matters at this hour. A plain-text notebook sits next to it for my thoughts.

Now

The current task in large type with its start and end time, a thin progress line for the hour, and the next three tasks. Empty slots stay blank.

Week

The whole week as a day-by-hour grid on one page, with today and the current hour highlighted. Tap any cell to fix a misread entry.

Import

Upload a timetable screenshot and a Claude vision model builds the week automatically. A built-in spending cap pauses each import at US$0.30; a typical one costs about US$0.01.

Note

A plain-text notebook that saves as you type, reached by swiping left from the bottom bar.

  • Platform Installable PWA for iPhone, full-screen and available offline
  • Privacy No accounts or servers. Schedule, notes and API key stay on the device; only the screenshot is sent to Anthropic for recognition
  • Limits No reminders or push notifications. Import needs a network connection and your own Claude API key
